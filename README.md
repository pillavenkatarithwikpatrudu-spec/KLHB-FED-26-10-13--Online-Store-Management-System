Abstract : 

The "Online Store Management System" is a centralized platform designed to simplify and improve the management of online store operations. Traditional and small-scale stores often depend on manual and disconnected methods for managing inventory, orders, customer information, payments, and sales records, which can result in errors, delays, and difficulty in tracking business performance.
The proposed system brings these operations together through a single platform. It provides "inventory management, order processing, customer management, sales analytics, secure payment processing, and an admin control panel". Real-time stock tracking and order updates help reduce manual errors, while sales analytics provide useful information about revenue, products, and sales trends.
Overall, the system provides store owners with better control and visibility over their business operations. It also provides opportunities for future improvements such as "AI-based product recommendations, a dedicated mobile application, and multi-vendor marketplace support".
